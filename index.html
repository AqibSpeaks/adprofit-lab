ROAS Calculator Guide

Understand your return on ad spend.

ROAS is revenue attributed to ads divided by advertising spend. A 4× ROAS means four dollars of attributed revenue per advertising dollar, not four dollars of profit.

What to keep in mind

Calculate your actual profit after advertising spend, fulfillment and overhead. Attribution is imperfect, so use ROAS alongside contribution margin and customer quality.

Break-even CPA Guide

Know your maximum sustainable acquisition cost.

Break-even advertising CPA is average order value minus variable costs and other per-order acquisition costs. If an order generates $100 and non-ad costs total $50, your break-even advertising CPA is $50.

What to keep in mind

Break-even is not the same as target CPA. Leave room for profit, returns, refunds and other costs. Recalculate when margins change.

Advertising Budget Planner Guide

Translate your monthly budget into a daily plan.

Divide the planned budget by campaign days to estimate daily spending. Divide total budget by target CPA for an illustrative acquisition count, not a forecast.

What to keep in mind

Actual CPA can vary due to audience, creative, seasonality and conversion tracking. Monitor spending and results rather than assuming the estimates are guaranteed.

UTM Builder Guide

Track campaign sources consistently.

UTM parameters such as utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign label traffic sources in analytics platforms. They help you compare campaign traffic.

What to keep in mind

Use consistent lowercase naming, check destination URLs and avoid placing personal information in UTM parameters. UTMs do not independently prove incremental conversions.
