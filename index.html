Create consistent UTM tracking links for campaigns and avoid common tagging mistakes.

How it works

UTM parameters are labels attached to destination URLs, such as utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign. They help analytics tools categorize incoming visits. Use consistent lowercase naming and test links before publishing.

What to keep in mind

Avoid including personal information in URL parameters. UTMs help classify traffic but do not themselves prove incremental conversions.
